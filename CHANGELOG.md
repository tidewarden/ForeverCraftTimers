# Changelog

## v0.1.1
- Character names no longer show a stray dash ("Suhx-" is now "Suhx").
- Shorter minimap tooltip: just your characters and their timers.

## v0.1.0
- First release: tracks Transmute, Mooncloth and Salt Shaker cooldowns on all your characters.
- Minimap button listing every character's timers; big on-screen alert when one is ready, on any character.
- Picks up cooldowns that are already running when you log in; `/fct set` to fix one by hand.
